We are pleased to submit our original research manuscript titled \textbf{"TURRET OS: Multi-Layered Graph and Rule-AST Architecture Rescuing Insider Threat Detection Against Out-of-Distribution Adversaries"} for consideration for publication as a full research article in \textit{Computers \& Security}.

\textbf{TURRET OS achieves a +0.2209 AUC rescue under the out-of-distribution (OOD) OCC-CP credential-phishing attack alongside 100\% ISO/IEC 27043 digital forensic readiness attribute coverage, bridging two previously disjoint research gaps---out-of-distribution detector collapse and evidence chain disconnection---into a single deployable decision and evidence pipeline.}

\textbf{Context and Problem Formulation:} \\
Insider threats remain one of the most critical security risks facing modern enterprise networks. Current machine-learning detectors rely heavily on single-layer feature novelty or off-hours behavioral anomalies. Consequently, they collapse when encountering out-of-distribution (OOD) credential-phishing adversaries operating during business hours with stolen credentials, low feature novelty, and removable-media transfers. Furthermore, existing detection platforms emit isolated machine-learning alerts that fail courtroom admissibility standards due to missing evidence chains.

\textbf{Key Contributions of TURRET OS:} \\
This manuscript presents \textbf{TURRET OS}, a four-layer joint-inference architecture that solves both challenges jointly:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.4em]
    \item \textbf{Cross-Format Metadata Extraction (L1)}: Ingests 10 document and telemetry formats (OOXML, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, PDF, DWG, EML, EPIC, Bitmap images, Git commits) to extract 12 derived feature columns and maintain a raw evidence ledger.
    \item \textbf{Provenance Knowledge Graph (L2)}: Materialises telemetry into a Neo4j knowledge graph adhering to W3C-PROV relational constraints.
    \item \textbf{Grammar-Driven Rule-AST Engine (L3)}: Evaluates 8 espionage rule families (R1--R8) in-tree at millisecond latency.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Layer GraphSAGE Fusion (L3.5)}: Combines L1 metadata with L2 graph topology under isotonic calibration and a runtime SHAP whitelist safety invariant that guarantees zero label leakage at deployment.
    \item \textbf{ISO/IEC 27043 Digital Forensic Readiness (L4)}: Emits Ed25519-signed W3C-PROV evidence packs with dual SHA-256 + BLAKE3 hashing over Merkle roots, achieving 100\% attribute coverage across all 8 forensic-readiness attributes.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Experimental Highlights:} \\
On the reproducible D3 TSEC benchmark (500 users, 365 days, 141k records), TURRET OS achieves \textbf{ROC-AUC 0.9997}, \textbf{F1@0.5\% FPR of 0.8774}, and \textbf{MCC 0.9770}. On the held-out D5 Synthetic-Tenant Pilot under an unobserved OOD exfiltrator, TURRET OS reports \textbf{ROC-AUC 0.9928} and \textbf{F1 0.8936}. Most importantly, under the OCC-CP credential-phishing attack, TURRET OS rescues single-layer detectors from a 26.25\% collapse down to a 4.12\% drop (within our $\le 5\%$ robustness bound), representing a \textbf{+22.09\% AUC architectural rescue}.
